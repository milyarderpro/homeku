## Relationship intelligence (INTERPRETATION / DESIGN PRINCIPLE)

1. **Color needs a material role.** Distinguish base wall color, roof, timber, exposed masonry, hardware and planting; don't apply one palette to every surface indiscriminately.
2. **Interior warmth versus exterior coolness is a theme, not a universal law.** Winter references such as `edh020` and `edh044` illustrate a pronounced contrast; `edh058` instead uses sunlit warmth both outside and within.
3. **Lighting is geometry-sensitive.** An imagined new room's light entry direction must correspond to actual positions of its windows; a camera viewpoint does not create a new sun-facing wall.
4. **Mood follows visible evidence.** Labels such as cozy, calm, inviting or rustic describe annotators' impressions; no viewer surveys or retention proof exist.
5. **Contrast supports readability.** Pale wall/dark opening combinations recur in multiple families, but roof type, massing and physical landscape still define the architectural identity.
6. **World continuity.** Weather appearance, exterior foliage, window views, temperature of practical lights and wall finishes belong to one coherent hypothetical home; visually incompatible climates or colors require a design reason.

**Boundaries:** color labels may depend on grading, white balance, compression and image synthesis. The archive gives no calibrated illumination levels or camera exposure measurements. Material names and season labels are visually inferred, not verified physically.
