# Design compatibility intelligence

These are **INFERENCES / DESIGN PRINCIPLES**, not recorded laws or proven associations with virality. They help reason about the coherence of an *original* building assembled from visual vocabulary.

## Compatibility dimensions

| Dimension | Compatible when… | Typical conflict to resolve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Massing + roof | Roof slopes, levels, bay widths and support volumes read as one structure | A tiny single-level cottage unexpectedly gains a villa-scale upper terrace |
| Material family | Render, stone, timber and metal share an intentional hierarchy | Three incompatible facade languages occupy the same small elevation |
| Window rhythm | Shapes, mullions, surrounds and height respond to floor/room geometry | A source's upper window is pasted over a roof pitch without an interior floor |
| Landscape + architecture | Entry, path, canopy and climate appearance fit the chosen mass | Snow-covered stone house opens onto tropical palm garden visible from same window |
| Interior + footprint | Seating, kitchen appliances, corridors and stairs fit the described scale | Full-size island and wide passage claim the same tiny-house aisle |
| Daylight + openings | Window views, shadows and interior lighting remain coherent | Same exterior wall has incompatible glazing and light direction between viewpoints |
| Framing + form | Composition reveals actual entry/roof/rooms without inventing geometry | A camera view implies a different building mass or newly appearing doorway |

## Evidence-grounded example families

- **Mediterranean appearance:** [edh058](../references/edh058/analysis.yaml), [edh030](../references/edh030/analysis.yaml), [edh050](../references/edh050/analysis.yaml): rendered warm walls, dark framed openings and warm tiled roof appearances recur with distinct envelopes. The palette can coexist with a new small footprint, but it does not automatically justify copying an upper balcony.
- **Storybook/stone:** [edh020](../references/edh020/analysis.yaml), [edh044](../references/edh044/analysis.yaml), [edh059](../references/edh059/analysis.yaml): expressive roof, masonry appearance and arched fenestration. If adapted to an original compact cottage, the roof form and window schedule must be rescaled coherently.
- **Compact open-plan:** [edh060](../references/edh060/analysis.yaml), [edh033](../references/edh033/analysis.yaml), [edh022](../references/edh022/analysis.yaml): spatial compression and shared room view, but not identical footprints or loft assumptions.
- **Tropical daylight:** [edh042](../references/edh042/analysis.yaml), [edh052](../references/edh052/analysis.yaml): landscape and openings relate to light; specific climate performance is unverified.

## Interpretation safeguards

Multiple reference IDs mean **multiple inspirations**, not evidence of one existing hybrid house. Compatibility depends on a newly established structural story; it cannot be inferred by concatenating three style names. When observations conflict, preserve their source identity and write an explicit new synthesis instead of changing the factual claim.

A strong hybrid feels like a distinct coherent house, not a collage of identifiable facade sections from different source videos.
