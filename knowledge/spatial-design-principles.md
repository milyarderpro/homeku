# Spatial Design Intelligence — coherence of one original house

**Important separation:** this document describes general design reasoning for hypothetical new homes. Source videos rarely supply full or measured floorplans. Treat `OBSERVATION`, `INFERENCE`, and `DESIGN_PRINCIPLE` as different things.

## What the sources really show

- **Observed combined rooms:** [edh060 shot-02](../references/edh060/analysis.yaml) describes an elongated living/kitchen/loft composition with stairs on the left and kitchen elements to the right/far end. [edh033 shot-02](../references/edh033/analysis.yaml) and [edh022 shot-02](../references/edh022/analysis.yaml) also label combined living/kitchen scenes. Do not extend this to all 58 homes.
- **Observed distinct scenes, adjacency unknown:** [edh058 shot-02](../references/edh058/analysis.yaml) is living; [edh058 shot-03](../references/edh058/analysis.yaml) is kitchen. The separate frames do not establish that the rooms share one wall, door, orientation or camera position.
- **Observed landscape through openings:** [edh058](../references/edh058/analysis.yaml) records vegetation and flower views through several interior openings; these constrain visual atmosphere, not compass direction or exact site plan.

## Design principles for a hypothetical *new* home

1. **Stable topological graph.** Define rooms as nodes, openings/passages as edges. If living connects directly to kitchen, the connecting opening must occur on the corresponding wall of both rooms, with compatible width and height.
2. **Stable orientation.** Identify front/back/left/right once in a house coordinate system. Do not treat camera-left as house-left when the viewpoint is reversed. Facing from living into kitchen and from kitchen into living must produce reciprocal views.
3. **Stable enclosing geometry.** Roof silhouette, exterior walls, window count/type, floor levels and chimney/porch position must be consistent with interior ceiling, stairs, and window locations.
4. **Functional clearances.** Room size must support the furniture plus plausible circulation. A kitchen island cannot occupy the same floor area as a sofa or permanently block the door.
5. **Separating open plan from adjacent rooms.** A kitchen may be visually adjacent to living in an open plan, or separated by an actual wall/door; the concept must make this explicit and remain unchanged from every viewpoint.
6. **Window-world reasoning.** The same window cannot show incompatible seasons or completely different terrain across views of the same house without a coherent explanation.
7. **Camera versus geometry.** Changing framing, perspective or focal point cannot teleport a doorway, remove a wall, flip a fireplace or swap the kitchen's position.
8. **No inferred source floorplan.** Unknown reference adjacency is `unknown`, never 'verified'; a newly invented adjacency is a creative design hypothesis.

## Abstract consistency example (new home, not a source plan)

If the kitchen is east of the living room and their shared opening lies in the living's east wall, then it must lie on the kitchen's west wall. Looking east from living may reveal kitchen; looking west from kitchen may reveal living **behind** the camera-facing kitchen zone, not arbitrarily in front of a completely different wall. A doorway or open-plan threshold can satisfy the connection; an unexplained wall removal cannot.

## Concept-level coherence ledger (knowledge schema, not a production form)

A defensible original-house model has: one floor-level graph; exterior footprint and entry; wall/window registry with positions; fixed features (stairs, fireplace, island, bath); furniture footprints; camera viewpoint coordinates; and landscape directions. Inconsistencies can be detected conceptually before any rendering. Exact dimensions remain estimates until designed.

**Scope:** the principles above are architectural reasoning. They are not claims that specific 58 video houses are physically built according to this diagram, or proof that a generator will obey them.
