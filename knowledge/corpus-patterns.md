## Design synthesis

- **INFERENCE — recurring identity cues:** repeated opening shapes, warm pale surfaces, timber-like accents and garden views can help readers recognize a visual family. This does not establish the same material or object across cuts. Compare [arched stone-like edh020](design-evidence/edh020.md), [organic edh013](design-evidence/edh013.md), and the rectilinear [edh060](design-evidence/edh060.md). The last is a counterexample to requiring arches.
- **DESIGN_PRINCIPLE — architecture and interior:** carry a selected geometry, opening family and palette into a new concept. Compatibility depends on proportions and functional circulation chosen for that new design; source edits do not supply its plan.
- **DESIGN_PRINCIPLE — landscape and light:** coordinate the outside-window world, exterior planting and selected light state. Compare snowy-looking [edh059](design-evidence/edh059.md) with green-garden [edh042](design-evidence/edh042.md). Appearance does not establish season, location, or an obligatory climate.
- **INFERENCE — space:** shared-function views can communicate compactness without implying a measured plan. [edh033](design-evidence/edh033.md) and [edh060](design-evidence/edh060.md) show useful loft examples. [edh058](design-evidence/edh058.md) presents functions in separate edited views; adjacency remains unknown.
- **INFERENCE — camera and composition:** in pilot samples, changing foreground occlusion and subject size provide cues to a changing viewpoint/framing. Three stills cannot distinguish all zoom, dolly, generated deformation or edit contributions. [VID-001](design-evidence/VID-001.md) ends its first sampled shot in transition blur, so its endpoint is not a clean architectural view.
- **DESIGN_PRINCIPLE — continuity and originality:** for a new house, choose consistent fixed anchors and geometry while varying composition and removable props deliberately. Combine principles from sources instead of reproducing a single source's entire signature. New relationships and material choices are creative decisions, not recovered source facts.
