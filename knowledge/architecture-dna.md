## Structural relationships: visually plausible synthesis

- **Massing precedes decoration.** Roof type, story count, facade width, entry location, porch and major windows jointly define a coherent silhouette. A dark roof and pale wall alone do not define a cottage.
- **Scale is a three-dimensional relation.** Perceived smallness depends on height, frontage, number of bays, ground footprint, entry/porch scale and surrounding garden; a distant view of a multi-level villa does not make it 'tiny'.
- **Openings have a family.** Align window head heights, frame colors, mullion rhythm, door scale and plausible wall support. Source examples include dark-framed arched windows ([edh058](../references/edh058/analysis.yaml), [edh044](../references/edh044/analysis.yaml)) and tall broad glazing ([edh042](../references/edh042/analysis.yaml)).
- **Material is appearance-based.** The YAML often distinguishes stucco/stone/wood *appearance*. No source establishes hidden construction layers or exact product names.
- **Roof and climate cues interact.** Snow-bearing pitched stone cottages ([edh020](../references/edh020/analysis.yaml), [edh059](../references/edh059/analysis.yaml)) and tropical deep-eave homes ([edh052](../references/edh052/analysis.yaml)) convey different visual environments; combining them requires an internally consistent new architectural rationale.

## Source distinctions worth preserving

[edh058](../references/edh058/analysis.yaml) is annotated `small`, single-story, front-entry gable; [edh060](../references/edh060/analysis.yaml) is a small trailer-based form with loft and upper terrace; [edh055](../references/edh055/analysis.yaml) is `large` and tropical. Their features should not be treated as size-equivalent. `architecture.building_scale.estimated_floor_area_m2` is low-confidence in examples and is not a permit-ready area calculation.

**Inference:** a distinctive roof silhouette paired with a restrained opening/material system is a stronger basis for original architecture than reusing a source's exact facade arrangement. See [originality framework](originality-framework.md).
